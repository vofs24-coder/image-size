Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Image Size respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Image Size website and image resizing tool.

1. Images You Process

Image Size is designed to process images directly in your web browser. Images selected for resizing are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our server for the purpose of resizing.

Because image processing happens in your browser, your original image remains on your device unless you choose to share or download the resulting file.

2. Information We May Receive

Image Size does not require you to create an account or provide personal information simply to resize an image.

However, normal technical information may be processed by our hosting provider or third-party services used by the website. This may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version

  • Operating system

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent mechanisms may be provided before certain types of advertising technologies are used.

5. Third-Party Services

Image Size may use third-party services for hosting, advertising, security, analytics or other website functions.

These third parties may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.
